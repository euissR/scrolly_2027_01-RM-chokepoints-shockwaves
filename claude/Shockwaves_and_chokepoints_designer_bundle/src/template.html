About this mockup

Each chapter is a geography (region) or a theme, made of a strategic overview and subsections on individual chokepoints (fixed places where traffic is squeezed) and shockwaves (disruptions that spread outward, shown by rings and by the places affected).

  • Strategic overviews (region and theme pages) are free-form and show how the places connect. On region pages, select a connection to see it drawn on the map. Switch on Guiding questions to see suggested prompts for authors.
  • Subsections (chokepoints and shockwaves) each answer the five guiding questions of the concept note and carry a few data points. The short headings are working titles; the original wording appears under each one when Guiding questions is on.
  • Theatre labels on the map are entry points; their pages list the subsections they contain.
  • All text and figures are placeholders to test the layout. Each needs replacing and sourcing by the responsible author.
  • All content sits in one data block at the top of the script, using plain latitude and longitude for locations.
